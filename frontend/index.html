Engine console — Action log

Every utterance becomes one Action object. This JSON is the contract both engines implement, and each committed entry here is one (audio, doc state) → action training triple for GemmaApollo.

In the real app this input is your microphone: VAD-chunked utterances go to the engine; “commit / scratch that / undo” are openWakeWord spotters handled instantly by the app. In mic mode the mic starts muted — say “Hey Jarvis” (or click Unmute) to start dictating; commit/undo/scratch keywords work even while muted. Dictation extends the pending equation piece by piece — say “commit” to finish the line, “scratch that” to remove the last piece. Raw LaTeX is accepted here as a stand-in for the speech→LaTeX stage. Click any committed line to edit it with MathQuill (Enter saves, Esc cancels).
